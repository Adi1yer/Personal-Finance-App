# Privacy Policy — Personal Finance

**Last updated:** September 18, 2026

Personal Finance is a **local-first** personal finance application. Your financial data is stored on your own computer. This policy explains what happens when you use optional Google Drive backup.

## Who we are

This app is developed and operated by the project owner for personal / private use. Contact: the email address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen (developer contact).

## Data we store on your device

- Account balances, transactions, and settings you enter or sync (e.g. via Plaid) are stored in a local database on your Mac.
- Encryption keys and API credentials you configure stay on your Mac (for example in local config files). They are not uploaded to a Personal Finance cloud server operated by us — **there is no central Personal Finance backend that hosts your ledger**.

## Google Drive backups (optional)

If you connect Google Drive:

- The app requests limited Google access (`drive.file`) so it can create and manage backup files **that this app creates**.
- Backups are zip files of your local ledger, uploaded to a folder such as **Personal Finance Backups** in **your** Google Drive.
- Google’s use of data is governed by [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not sell your data. We do not use Drive backups for advertising.

## Plaid and other third parties

If you link a bank with Plaid, Plaid’s privacy policy applies to that connection. Tokens are stored encrypted on your device.

## Data retention

- Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall/remove the app data.
- Drive backups remain in your Google Drive until you delete them or disconnect and remove them yourself.

## Your choices

- You can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time.
- You can revoke app access at [Google Account → Third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- You can delete local profile data and Drive backup files manually.

## Changes

We may update this policy by changing this document in the project repository. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
