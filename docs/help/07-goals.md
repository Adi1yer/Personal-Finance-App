# 7. Annual goals

Open **Goals**.

## Investing goal (e.g. invest 20% of income)

1. Set the target **percent of income** (or absolute amount if your build supports it).
2. Ensure income is flowing into tracked accounts (paycheck deposits via Plaid, or categorize income correctly).
3. Contributions count from:
   - Detected investment contributions / matches / deposits / transfers-in
   - Manual **Total contributions** on accounts that don’t sync those transactions
4. Read the **per-account breakdown** under the investing goal to see what is and isn’t counted.

### What “on track” means

Progress = YTD invested ÷ (YTD income × target %). If income isn’t imported yet, progress will look wrong — fix income sync or categories first.

## Safety-net / emergency fund goals

1. Set a target months of expenses or dollar amount.
2. Map which cash accounts count toward the safety net (if the UI offers account selection).
3. Update cash balances via Sync or manual balance updates.

## Tips

- Revisit Goals after each Sync and after updating Total contributions.
- Ask Advisor: “Am I on track for my investing goal?” once Ollama is running.
